## Where to find the release

- Sliders control / Your account: colour personalisation and accessibility.
- Training: richer logger, rotation, template versions/copies, exercise trends and private video comparison.
- Calories / Recipes: serving units, diary reuse, targets, recipe arithmetic and pantry tools.
- Home: habits, weekly consistency and the visible device sync queue.
- Import: reviewed column mapping, locale confirmation, resumable batches and safe batch rollback.
- Connections: privacy/media tools, release centre, personal feature switches and private feedback records.

## Verification limits

Local Chromium tests do not certify iPhone Safari, Android Chrome, screen readers, native health integrations, closed-app push, billing or production account recovery. Existing optional AI/R2 integrations remain. No new paid provider was introduced.

## Training completion update — 23 October 2026

Completed roadmap items 038, 041, 042 and 046: configurable account-synced rep ranges; muscle drill-down with rolling date windows; weighted/direct/indirect views; and simultaneous R/L trend comparison. The comparison reads actual side loads, gym pins, reps and completion, including a completed side of a partial pair. Legacy shared copies are excluded from independent-side charts unless explicitly included and labelled. Missing sides remain gaps. Default heat-map scoring remains 1 direct / 0.5 indirect.

The broader roadmap remains incomplete: 55 partial, 25 pending, and 8 requiring platform/operational verification. The status table is a completion audit, not a claim that native Apple Health access or every PDF feature is finished.
